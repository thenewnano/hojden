# New technical manager and updated procedures from September 1st

The board of BRF Höjden would like to inform the residents of Sjupundsgatan 27, 31 & 35 that we will have a new technical manager from September 1st, 2024. A.R.C Fastighetspartner AB will take over Stena Fastigheter's responsibility and handle all service requests and questions regarding your apartments and the property. This also includes warranty matters which will then be forwarded to Stena Fastigheter.

**Contact information A.R.C Fastighetspartner AB:**

* Switchboard: 010-551 47 00
* Responsible for the property - Johannes Lindqvist: 010-551 47 34
* Website and fault reporting: <https://arcfast.se/>

**Important to note:** Stena Fastigheter will continue to be responsible for the common areas in Ekebäckshöjd outside the stairwells, such as courtyards, garages, roof terrace, and lounge. For matters concerning these areas, please contact the board who will then forward the matter to Stena Fastigheter if necessary.

**In case of urgent matters concerning the common areas which are Stena Fastigheter's responsibility, contact Stena Fastigheter's emergency number directly at 0771-80 50 60 or 031-718 00 08.**
